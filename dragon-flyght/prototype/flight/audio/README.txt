Optional real recordings for Dragon Flyght.
Drop files here with these exact names (mp3, ogg, wav or m4a). They loop, and fade in when that scene is playing:

  tornado     freight-train roar, wind, debris
  hurricane   howling wind, driving rain, surf
  flood       rushing water, heavy rain
  wildfire    fire roar and crackle
  earthquake  deep rumble, rattling, falling debris
  city        calm city ambience for a normal day

Missing files are skipped and the built-in synthesized sound is used. When a recording is present the
built-in bed for that scene gets quieter so they do not fight.
Use files you are allowed to use (check each site's license). Short loops (30-90 s) that tile smoothly work best.
Free libraries: freesound.org, pixabay.com/sound-effects, and others; licenses vary per file.
Reload the page after adding files. Sound mix levels are in js/audio.js (the LEVEL line).
